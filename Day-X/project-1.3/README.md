## 📅 Day 10 – Project Completion 🚀

Today marks the **successful completion** of the *Gett Ride Failure Analysis & Optimization* project! 🎉  

Over the past 10 days, I’ve built and finalized an end-to-end analytics workflow using **Microsoft Fabric, PySpark, Delta Lake, and SQL**. The project is now complete and delivers actionable insights for improving ride-hailing efficiency.

### ✅ Key Achievements
- Designed **Bronze → Silver → Gold** data architecture for clean, reliable analytics.
- Analyzed **31,268 failed ride requests** to uncover root causes.
- Identified that **73.24% of failures occurred before driver assignment**.
- Discovered the **critical 90-second intervention window** for customer retention.
- Conducted **shift-level analysis** to highlight high-risk cancellation periods.
- Proposed **strategic recommendations** for:
  - Faster driver matching
  - Targeted driver incentives
  - Pre-positioning drivers before peak demand
  - Improved ETA communication

### 📊 Business Impact
- Framework to **reduce cancellations** and increase ride completion.
- Strategies to **balance driver supply and demand**.
- Potential to **boost revenue** by saving high-risk rides.
- Enhanced **customer experience** through better communication and reduced uncertainty.

### 🧪 Next Steps
- Run **A/B tests** on interventions (90-sec updates, targeted discounts, driver repositioning).
- Measure **incremental completed rides vs. cost of intervention**.
- Scale successful strategies across the platform.
