## 📚 Key Learnings

- Building reports is much more than dragging visuals—understanding the business questions behind each KPI is essential.
- Semantic Models simplify report development by organizing relationships between fact and dimension tables.
- Choosing the right visualization is as important as writing the correct SQL query.
- Good dashboards should tell a business story, not just display numbers.
